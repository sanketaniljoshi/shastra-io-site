The Shastra method

A clear path from evidence to action.

Every engagement runs on the same three-part rhythm. It's how we keep work focused, measurable and worth the spend — regardless of the discipline we're leading with.

01

Verify

Verify the decision, evidence, constraints and owner before production starts. Depending on the brief, this may be a three-business-day readiness sprint or a longer structured audit.

02

Build

Create the agreed strategy, creative or system in visible stages, with named approvals and an evidence trail.

03

Learn

Measure what happened, separate observation from interpretation, and decide whether to scale, revise, wait or stop.

Good to know

Questions we get before the first call.

The things worth knowing before you write to us — answered plainly, including the ones where the honest answer is "it depends".

What exactly does Shastra AI do?

Shastra works across AI builds, creative systems, performance and emerging channels, and brand strategy. Every engagement begins with one defined commercial decision and a fixed scope. The current specialist entry offer is a three-business-day ChatGPT Ads readiness sprint for eligible advertisers.

Are you an agency, or a product company?

Shastra is a founder-led services business that also builds products and internal systems. It is a unit of Hridtech Private Limited. Client work is scoped separately from Shastra's affiliated ventures, and internal examples are labelled as such.

Where are you based, and which markets do you work in?

Shastra is based between Bengaluru and Kent and considers work in India, the UAE, the UK and the US. Market, tax, platform and delivery conditions are verified for each engagement before commitment.

Who will actually do the work?

The founder remains accountable for scope, decisions and delivery. Where a brief needs a specialist, that role and responsibility are disclosed in the engagement scope rather than presented as an anonymous team.

How does an engagement run?

Verify, build and learn. We first confirm the decision, evidence and constraints; then build the agreed work in visible stages; then measure the result and recommend whether to scale, revise, wait or stop. The timing depends on the scope.

Do you use AI to replace human judgment?

No. AI changes how fast you can find out what works; it does not change what makes a brand work. We use it for research, ideation, asset generation and testing at scale, and we keep senior creative direction and strategic judgment on the human side. That is why the output should not feel commoditised.
