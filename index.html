What we do

Four ways we help brands compound.

Each engagement starts with what the brand actually needs — not with a fixed deliverable. These are the disciplines we lead with.

01

AI Build

Websites, apps, internal tools, automations and AI agents. We embed AI into the product surfaces and operational flows that matter most — from customer-facing copilots to back-of-house process redesign.

02

Creative & Content

Campaigns, content production, design systems, social. AI-assisted ideation and asset generation at scale, paired with senior creative direction so the output feels considered, not commoditised.

03

Performance Marketing

Paid media across Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok and emerging channels. AI-driven creative testing, audience modelling and budget allocation that finds your unit economics faster and scales them harder.

Brand & Strategy

Who we work with

Brands ready to move with intent.

We work with founders, marketers and operators across stages and sectors — wherever AI changes what's possible. A few patterns of who we serve best:

Challenger brands

Early-stage consumer and B2B brands building from scratch — typically founder-led, Series A to C, looking to compete with incumbents through speed and craft rather than budget.

Established brands modernising

Mid-market to enterprise teams whose existing marketing function predates the AI shift — and whose CMOs need to rebuild the operating model without disrupting the in-flight work.

Operators with a thesis

PE and growth-equity teams with a portfolio company that needs the brand, marketing or product surface re-tooled before the next round — fast, with senior people, on a fixed timeline.

Cross-border launches

Brands launching into India from the UAE/UK/US, or out of India into Western markets. We work fluently across both sides — culturally, operationally and from a media-buying standpoint.

The Shastra method

A clear path from signal to scale.

Every engagement runs on the same three-part rhythm. It's how we keep work focused, measurable and worth the spend — regardless of the discipline we're leading with.

01

Understand

Brand audit, audience research, competitive landscape, AI capability mapping. Two to three weeks of structured discovery so the work that follows is built on signal, not guesswork. Output: a strategy artefact your team can act on.

02

Build

Strategy, creative, systems — executed with AI in the loop from the first sprint, not bolted on at the end. Senior people on the work, daily writes, weekly reviews. You see what we make as we make it.

03

Scale

Measurement, optimisation, growth loops, hand-off. The point isn't a deck — it's a live system your team can run. We stay on as long as we're adding value, and we don't pretend otherwise.
