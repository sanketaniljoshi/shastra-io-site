Before you book

Useful answers.

Can Shastra get us access to ChatGPT Ads?

No. Shastra assesses evidence visible in the client's own account and cannot grant or guarantee access.

Do you need our password?

No. The review is screen-led in the client's account. Never send passwords, payment-card information, API keys, account exports, customer lists or private ChatGPT conversations.

Does the sprint include campaign launch?

No. It ends with a readiness decision and evidence-backed directions. Build, activation and optimisation require a separate full-pilot agreement and client approval.

Is the ₹15,000 credited later?

Yes, when the full pilot is signed within 14 calendar days of sprint delivery.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Shastra does not guarantee access, approval, placement, delivery, clicks, conversions or financial performance.

What if the decision is WAIT or NO-GO?

The memo identifies the evidence, gaps and recommended next action. The sprint fee pays for that decision work and is not contingent on a GO result.
