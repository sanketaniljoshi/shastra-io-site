Sanket Anil Joshi

Founder, Shastra AI

Sanket Anil Joshi — also credited as Sanket Joshi — is the founder of Shastra AI, the AI technology company of Hridtech Private Limited. Shastra works across four disciplines: AI build, creative and content, performance marketing, and brand and strategy, with offices in Bangalore and Kent.

A British national and an alumnus of London Business School and Oxford Brookes University, he spent over a decade in investment banking across European markets before returning to build in India. He has lectured on finance and entrepreneurship at Indian business schools, including a career session on investment banking at SIMSREE, Mumbai, and a 2024 guest lecture on entrepreneurship at ISBR College, Bengaluru.

Shastra AI is the shared engine behind Hridtech's other ventures:

  • CourPro — practical, project-led AI and skills education
  • Skilyo — verified AI and tech talent, matched with employers
  • MPS Aerospace — aviation training, Bengaluru
  • Content Co. — media and content, home to Katha Rahasya

Earlier, he founded Syahee.com (now Facestorys.com), a blogging and social platform for literature, and organised the first-ever Vadodara Literature Festival in February 2017 across six languages. He writes mythology fiction under the Shastra imprint.
