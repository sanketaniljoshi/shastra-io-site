2. What we collect

  • Enquiries: your name, email address, company, and the contents of any message you send us about a project or engagement.

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss possible work.

  • To understand how the site is used, and to keep it working and secure.

Lawful bases (UK GDPR): your consent where you contact us or agree to analytics, our legitimate interests in operating and improving our business, and performance of a contract where we are delivering work for you. In India we rely on your consent under the DPDP Act 2023.

4. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf — website hosting, email, and analytics. Where a project requires a third-party AI or cloud provider, we tell you before sharing your material with it. We do not sell your data, and we do not use your project material to train public AI models.

5. International transfers

Because we operate in the United Kingdom and India, your data may be transferred between these countries and stored with providers outside them. We use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

6. Retention

We keep enquiry correspondence for up to 24 months from your last contact with us. Client project data is kept for the duration of the engagement and for as long afterwards as we need it for our records or the law requires, then deleted or anonymised.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to give you access to your data, correct it, delete it, or provide a copy, and you can withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@shastra.io with the subject "Privacy request". If you are in the UK you may complain to the Information Commissioner's Office; if you are in India you may approach the Data Protection Board.

8. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure site usage. It sets cookies in your browser. You can block or delete these through your browser settings, or install Google's opt-out add-on, without losing access to anything on this site.

9. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encrypted connections and access controls on the systems we use.

10. Children

This site and our services are intended for business use and are not directed at anyone under 18.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The "last updated" date above will change, and we will notify you of material changes where it is appropriate to do so.
